# STYLE v2 "hook-first" (started 2026-11-21, authorised pivot)

Why: Nov 19-21 videos got 0-1 views. Diagnosis: first frame looked like a loading spinner on black, the same Bitcoin icon appeared on unrelated topics, 8 near-identical posts/day, and nothing in the first second told a viewer why to stay.

Rules for every video from now on:
1. Scene 1 is ALWAYS `icon: "hook"` with `hook` (<=60 chars): a shocking/curious claim or question in plain words ("ONE CLICK CAN EMPTY YOUR WALLET", "YOUR EXCHANGE DOES NOT OWN YOUR COINS"). Last line is shown in the accent `color` (red = danger, green = fix, yellow = curiosity, cyan = info). The hook is fully readable from frame 0 (it is the TikTok/Shorts cover). Narration of scene 1 = the same hook said out loud, max 12 words.
2. Total 20-35 seconds: 3-5 scenes, narration 12-25 words each. Cut every filler word.
3. Use `hook` scenes for the key claims (problem, the trick, the fix). Use illustrated icons ONLY when they truly match the topic (question, checklist, gauge, chartdown, key, wallet, scam, safe). Never reuse an icon that shows something unrelated (e.g. bank/Bitcoin on a token-approval topic).
4. Last scene: a hook scene with a concrete action ("CHECK YOUR APPROVALS TODAY", color green), then outro with foot "Educational only. Not financial advice." (outro max 3 s).
5. Titles/captions: curiosity or danger + beginner promise, first 40 chars carry the hook. Ask one question at the end of the caption to invite comments.
6. Frequency: max 3 videos/day, spaced; vary topic type (scam warning / myth vs fact / "what happens if" / 1 number that shocks). Never two similar topics in a row.
7. Review again on 2026-12-05 using STATS.csv and the daily report; if views per video are still < 50 on TikTok, change the topic mix, not just the visuals.
